FitCoach privacy policy

FitCoach is a private coaching app used by a small group of friends and their trainer. It is not sold or advertised. Last updated 5 November 2026.

What we collect

  • Your profile: name, email, phone, date of birth, sex, height, weight, goal, diet preference and any injuries you tell us about.
  • Food photos, food logs, workout logs, body measurements and progress photos you add.
  • From Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iPhone, through a Shortcut you install): active calories burned, total calories burned, steps and sleep for the last 7 days. FitCoach only reads this data and never writes to your health data.

Who can see it

  • You, your trainer and the app admin. No other people.
  • Health data is used only to show calories burnt, steps and sleep to you and your trainer.
  • We never sell or share your data with advertisers.

AI services we use

  • Meal photos and descriptions are sent to Google's Gemini AI to estimate calories and may be used by Google to improve its services.
  • To write your insights, weekly summaries and meal ideas, your first name, goal, diet preference, foods to avoid and your food, workout, weight and activity numbers are sent to Groq AI, with Google's Gemini AI as a backup.
  • If you agree to the AI coach, your logs, meal photos and health conditions are also reviewed by Google's Gemini AI to coach you, and may be used by Google to improve its services.
  • These AI services may run outside India.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in a private Supabase database in Seoul, South Korea, protected so that each person can only reach the data they are allowed to see.

Meal photos are deleted from the app after 7 days. Encrypted weekly backups, which only the admin can open, keep copies of photos for up to 35 days and of other data for up to 90 days.

Your choices

  • You can disconnect Health Connect at any time in its settings, or delete the iPhone Shortcut.
  • You can ask your trainer or the admin to delete all your data at any time. Encrypted backups of it are removed within 90 days.

Health

Calories and nutrition numbers are estimates, not medical advice. Please check with a doctor before starting a new exercise or diet programme.
