FitCoach on iPhone

Setup guide · about 15 minutes

1Add FitCoach to your Home Screen

On iPhone, FitCoach is a web app – no App Store needed. It must be added to the Home Screen so that notifications work.

  1. Open this link in Safari (not Chrome or the Gmail browser):
    fitcoach-ten-nu.vercel.app
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow, at the bottom or top of the screen).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen → Add.
  4. Close Safari and open FitCoach from the new icon on your Home Screen. Always use this icon from now on.

If the link opened inside Gmail or WhatsApp, tap the Safari/compass icon or "Open in Safari" first. Needs iOS 16.4 or newer for notifications (Settings → General → About → iOS Version).

2Log in

  1. Type the email address this guide was sent to and tap Send code.
  2. Check your email for a 6-digit code (look in Junk too) and type it in. iPhone often offers the code above the keyboard – just tap it.
  3. You stay logged in – you won't need a code every time.

"No account found"? Ask your trainer or admin – only they can add people.

3First-time setup

  1. Read and accept the consent screen (what we collect and who can see it).
  2. Fill in the short profile: date of birth, height, weight, goal, food preference, any injuries or health conditions.

4Turn on notifications

  1. In the Home Screen app, go to Me → Notifications → Turn on notifications and tap Allow.
  2. Tap Send test notification – you should get one in a few seconds.
  3. Set your meal reminder times on the same screen.

No "Turn on" button or nothing arrives? You are probably in Safari, not the Home Screen app – go back to step 1.

5Connect calories burnt (Apple Health)

A small Apple Shortcut sends your active calories, resting calories and steps from Apple Health (and your Apple Watch, if you have one) to FitCoach.

  1. In FitCoach go to Me → Health sync.
  2. Tap Get Shortcut → Add Shortcut. Don't rename it – it must stay FitCoach Sync.
  3. Back in FitCoach tap Connect, then Finish in Shortcuts. The Shortcut opens, saves your code and syncs.
  4. The first time, iPhone asks for permission – tap Allow for Health and Always Allow for sending data to FitCoach.

Make it run by itself every day

  1. Open the Shortcuts app → Automation tab → + (New Automation).
  2. Choose Time of Day → 10:00 PM, Daily, and Run Immediately → Next.
  3. Pick FitCoach Sync → Done.
  4. Repeat for 7:00 AM (this completes the previous day).

You can also tap Sync now on the calories card any time. No Apple Watch? Your iPhone still counts steps and active calories while you carry it.

6Daily use

  • Food: tap Food → Log a meal, take a photo or type what you ate. Check the estimate, fix anything, then save.
  • Workouts: open Workouts to see today's plan and log your sets.
  • Today: calories eaten vs your target, calories burnt, water and your next session.
  • Ask coach (if you have the AI coach): ask anything about food and training.

The app updates itself – nothing to install.

Problem? In the app go to Me → Report a problem, or message your trainer.

Calories are estimates. Please check with your doctor before starting a new exercise or diet programme.
