1Today

  • Calories ring: what you've eaten against your daily target, with protein, carbs and fat bars.
  • Calories burnt: from your phone or watch (or estimated from your workouts), and your net balance for the day. Tap Sync now on iPhone to update it.
  • Water: tap + for every glass (250 ml).
  • Today's workout and your next session – tap Join for an online session.
  • Your week in review: a short summary your trainer shares with you.

2Food – log what you eat

  1. Tap Food → Log a meal.
  2. Choose how:
    • Photo – take a picture of your plate. Pick Quick estimate (~20 s) or Detailed analysis (~30 s, more careful).
    • Type it – e.g. "2 chapati, 1 cup dal, salad".
    • Manual – enter the calories yourself.
  3. Check the result. Use ×0.5 / ×1 / ×1.5 / ×2 to change a portion, or tap an item to edit it. Changed the name or portion? Tap Re-estimate.
  4. If FitCoach asks a question (like "How many chapatis?"), tap an answer.
  5. Set the meal and time, then Save.

Calories are estimates – always check and correct them. Up to 15 AI estimates a day. Only photograph your food; meal photos are deleted after 7 days.

Food ideas for your week: when your trainer shares meal ideas, you'll find them under Food.

3Workouts

  • See your trainer's plan and today's day. Tap an exercise for tips on doing it right.
  • Log each set: reps and kg (or minutes for cardio and yoga). You'll see what you did last time, and a rest timer starts after each set.
  • For cardio machines you can add the machine's kcal.
  • My workouts: build your own routines or use Quick log for extra training.
  • History and personal bests show your progress.

4Stats

  • Pick 7, 30 or 90 days: average calories against your target, days on target, streak, net balance and weight change.
  • Eating patterns: usual meal times, late dinners, skipped breakfasts, your most-eaten foods and your biggest calorie sources.
  • Insights: a few lines from the AI on what's going well and one thing to focus on.

5Me

  • Progress: add your weight and waist, and progress photos (private, once a week). See charts for weight, calories and workouts.
  • Notifications and reminders: turn on notifications, set your meal and workout reminder times, send a test.
  • Health sync: connect Health Connect (Android) or the Apple Health Shortcut (iPhone).
  • My sessions and Weekly summaries.
  • Edit my details (goal, diet, injuries, foods to avoid), Language (English / ಕನ್ನಡ) and Time zone.
  • Report a problem – sends a message (and a screenshot, if you add one) straight to the admin.

6Ask coach (AI coach)

If you have the AI coach or your trainer uses an AI assistant, you'll see a coach card on Today. Agree once, then:

  • Tap Ask coach and ask about food and training – it knows your recent meals, workouts and goals.
  • You get a short morning and evening note, and the coach may set today as a workout, light or rest day.
  • It may check and correct meal estimates – you'll see "Checked by coach" on the meal, and bigger changes come to you as a suggestion to accept or keep yours.
  • Health, medicine or supplement questions go to a person (your trainer or the admin). For chest pain, dizziness or breathlessness, stop and call your doctor or 108.

1Clients

Clients who need attention come first, with flags such as: no food logged in 2 days, no workout in 3+ days, eating well below or above target, missed session, weekly summary ready.

Tap a client to open their page:

  • Overview – profile, last health sync, AI assistant on/off.
  • Food – their meals with photos (while available) and calories burnt.
  • Workouts – what they logged; Log a workout for them after a session together.
  • Targets – calories, protein, carbs, fat and water. FitCoach suggests an estimated starting point.
  • Plan – assign a workout plan.
  • Reminders – meal and workout times, lock them, add your own reminders (cardio, food, other).
  • Summary – the AI drafts a weekly summary every Monday; edit it and Share.
  • Meal ideas – draft a week of short meal ideas, edit, then Share.
  • Stats and Progress – their trends, weight and photos.

2Plans

  • Create a plan → add days → add exercises with sets, reps and rest. Reorder, duplicate and assign to clients.
  • Use the shared exercise library (gym, home and yoga) or add your own with a demo link.
  • Plan days rotate: the client's next day follows the last one they logged.

3Sessions

  • Connect your Google account once to get Google Meet links and calendar invites automatically (or paste your own link, or choose in person with a place).
  • Create one-off or repeating sessions, pick clients – they get a notification and a Join button, plus a reminder 15 minutes before.
  • After the session, mark who was present or missed.

4AI assistant (optional)

Switch on AI assistant on a client's Overview. Every morning the head coach reviews them and suggests changes (meal fixes, the day's plan, notes). You approve them under Clients → AI assistant – Approve all, one by one, or let small changes apply by themselves. Your plan stays yours.

5My fitness

Track your own food, workouts and calories just like a client. Only you and the admin can see it.

Tips & privacy

  • No internet? Meals, sets, water and burn entries are saved on your phone ("Saved offline – will sync") and sent when you're back online. Photo estimates need internet – type the meal instead.
  • Tap the refresh button at the top of any screen to get the latest data.
  • Who sees your data: you, your trainer and the admin – nobody else. Photos are analysed by Google's Gemini AI.
  • You can ask the admin for a copy of your data, or to delete everything, any time.
  • Not getting reminders on Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, Realme or OnePlus? Set FitCoach's battery to Unrestricted (see the Android guide).

Problem? In the app go to Me → Report a problem, or message your trainer.

Calories are estimates. FitCoach does not give medical advice – please check with your doctor before starting a new exercise or diet programme.
