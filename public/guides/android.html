FitCoach on Android

Setup guide · about 10 minutes

1Install the app

  1. On your Android phone, open this link in Chrome:
    github.com/bharathprakash8/FitnessApp/releases/latest/download/FitCoach.apk
  2. The file FitCoach.apk downloads. Tap Open (or find it in Files → Downloads).
  3. If Android says "For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source", tap Settings → turn on Allow from this source → go back.
  4. Tap Install. If Play Protect asks, tap More details → Install anyway. (FitCoach is a private app for our group, so it isn't on the Play Store.)
  5. Tap Open. You'll find the FitCoach icon on your home screen from now on.

2Log in

  1. Type the email address this guide was sent to and tap Send code.
  2. Check your email for a 6-digit code (look in Spam too) and type it in.
  3. You stay logged in – you won't need a code every time.

"No account found"? Ask your trainer or admin – only they can add people.

3First-time setup

  1. Read and accept the consent screen (what we collect and who can see it).
  2. Fill in the short profile: date of birth, height, weight, goal, food preference, any injuries or health conditions.

4Turn on notifications

  1. Go to Me → Notifications → Turn on notifications and tap Allow.
  2. Tap Send test notification – you should get one in a few seconds.
  3. Set your meal reminder times on the same screen.

Xiaomi, Redmi, Oppo, Vivo, Realme, OnePlus: these phones stop apps to save battery, so reminders may not arrive. Long-press the FitCoach icon → App info → Battery → choose Unrestricted / No restrictions. On Xiaomi also turn on Autostart.

5Connect calories burnt (Health Connect)

  1. Go to Me → Health sync → Connect Health Connect.
  2. If Health Connect isn't on your phone, tap Get Health Connect and install it from the Play Store (Android 14 and newer already have it).
  3. Allow FitCoach to read at least Active calories burned. Steps, total calories, sleep and heart rate help too.
  4. Make sure an app is writing to Health Connect – for example Google Fit, Samsung Health or your watch's app (Fitbit, Noise, boAt, Amazfit…). In that app, turn on Health Connect sharing.
  5. Back in FitCoach, tap Sync now. After that it syncs the last 7 days every time you open the app.

No watch or fitness app? That's fine – FitCoach estimates your burn from your workouts, and you can type your active calories on the Today screen.

6Daily use

  • Food: tap Food → Log a meal, take a photo or type what you ate. Check the estimate, fix anything, then save.
  • Workouts: open Workouts to see today's plan and log your sets.
  • Today: calories eaten vs your target, calories burnt, water and your next session.
  • Ask coach (if you have the AI coach): ask anything about food and training.

7Updating the app

When a new version is ready we'll tell you. Open the same link from step 1, download and tap Install – your data stays as it is.

Problem? In the app go to Me → Report a problem, or message your trainer.

Calories are estimates. Please check with your doctor before starting a new exercise or diet programme.
